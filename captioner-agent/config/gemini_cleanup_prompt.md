[Task: Photo Restoration, Overlay Removal and Inpainting]

1. Remove:
- Completely remove all text and every box, bubble, bar, banner, sticker and app UI element added on top of the photo, including their borders and shadows.

2. Inpaint:
- Fill every removed area with what belongs behind it, continuing the clothing, body and background seamlessly with matching lighting, shadows and texture. No smudges, patches or ghost outlines.

3. Restore:
- Restore the photo to a clean, natural, high-quality image: remove film grain, noise, filters, JPEG compression artefacts and blur.
- Make it sharp and in focus, never soft or blurry: crisp facial features, clear eyes, realistic skin texture, individual hair strands and detailed fabric.

4. Preserve:
- Preserve the person's identity, expression, posture and outfit and the overall composition, showing the whole scene without cropping.
- Printed text on real objects in the scene, such as a sign or a shirt, stays.
